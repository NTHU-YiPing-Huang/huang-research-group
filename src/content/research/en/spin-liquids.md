Fractionalization, emergent gauge structure, and physical-spin descriptions of quantum matter.

The Kitaev Hamiltonian is

$$
H=-\sum_{\langle ij\rangle_\gamma}
J_\gamma \sigma_i^\gamma\sigma_j^\gamma.
$$
